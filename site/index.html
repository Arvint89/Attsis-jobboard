A lightweight, deterministic starter kit. Every card is a template — no LLM required. The Tailor tab is where paid, reviewed material lives.

Company brief template

Before every interview, fill this in from the company's website (5–10 min):

• What they build (1 sentence)
• Who buys it (customer segment)
• Their competitors (2–3)
• Latest news/funding round
• Something specific to ask about the role

Technical questions

Warm up on the fundamentals for your title. Practice by explaining out loud, not silently in your head.

• A recent project — 90-second walkthrough
• A hard bug you fixed and how
• A trade-off decision and its cost
• Something you'd do differently in hindsight

Behavioural questions

Use the STAR pattern: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Have 3–4 rehearsed stories.

• Conflict with a colleague — resolved
• Missed a deadline — recovery
• Led a change others resisted
• Failure and what you learned

Salary + negotiation

Know your number before the call. Base salary bands come from levels.fyi, Glassdoor, and Blind.

• Your desired base (research-backed)
• Your walk-away number
• Total comp: base + bonus + equity + benefits
• Never disclose current salary first
